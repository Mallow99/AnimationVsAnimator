Summary Notes

His notes, in his words: things about you, things that happened, his opinions. With an AI brain on, he writes his own and every so often the AI tidies them into the summary. Offline, he still counts what you do to him and remembers it. Click a note to edit it; your edits are his memories now. Saved in memory.json next to his settings.

With him

His tools and worn gear. Right-click him to take something, then drag it or drop it back onto him. Put away returns it to the inventory below. Helmet and boots are optional; he starts with just his pen.

Inventory

Everything he could have. Drop it in drops it from the top of the screen near him and he goes to get it; Give him puts it on his belt; Wear equips his helmet or boots. Your own items (below) show up here too.

Props

Drag furniture to arrange his desktop. He can sit, watch TV, play video games, ride and paint. Pick Play Othello on the TV and he'll grab a controller and invite you; the game has its own movable window, and Talk to him opens chat while the match continues.

Make your own

Every item is a small .json file. Copy one of the examples in the folder (pen.json, wooden-sword.json, mace.json...), change the name, the length, the colors and the shape, save it, then click Reload items. The step-by-step guide is MAKING-THINGS.md in the folder (and in the project's docs folder).
