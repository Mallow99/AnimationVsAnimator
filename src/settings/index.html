Summary Notes

His notes, in his words: things about you, things that happened, his opinions. With an AI brain on, he writes his own and every so often the AI tidies them into the summary. His belt

He wears a belt with three slots: left hip, right hip, and his back. His pen lives on his hip (he draws with it) and his wooden sword on his back. Right-click him on the desktop to take something; it dangles from your cursor (swing it at him if you're feeling mean). Click him to give it back, or click anywhere else to drop it. He'll ask for his things back, and pick them up if they're lying around.
