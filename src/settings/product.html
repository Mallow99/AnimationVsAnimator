What’s included
  • One to five buddies with their own belongings, moods and friendships.
  • Petting, picking up, tossing, cartoon sparring and shared activities.
  • Reading, drawing, catch, TV, handheld games, Pong and Othello.
  • Names, colours, size, personality, appearance and movement customization.
  • Furniture, supplies and file-based custom items and props.
  • Local conversations, branching stories, saved progress and backup export.
  • Optional window climbing, sounds and mischief; Pause, Hide and Quit.

Works offline without an account or AI key. Built-in conversations cover familiar topics, not general-purpose answers.

Your data stays here

No account, advertising, analytics or AI service. Conversations, memories and custom things live on this computer. Chat export is in Buddies. Updates are manual and preserve your saves.

Damaged saves recover automatically from their last-good copy. Full exports can be used for recovery with the included guide.
