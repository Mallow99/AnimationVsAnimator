# Trying Arnold

Download the ZIP for your computer and extract the entire ZIP first. On Windows,
open Mallow-Desk-Buddies.exe inside the extracted folder. On Apple Silicon Mac,
open Mallow-Desk-Buddies.app; follow INSTALL.md for the unsigned test build.

1. Open Settings from the tray/menu-bar icon.
2. In the Settings tab, enter **ARNOLD** in Bonus code and select Unlock buddy.
3. Select Meet Arnold. If five buddies are already out, uncheck one first, then
   check Arnold. Everyone keeps their saved belongings and memories.
4. Arnold has **JT** on his arm and a feather quill. Ask him “draw” or choose
   Draw something in Activities. Try “tell me a joke” in his conversation.

He is talkative and cheerfully unfunny. His name, colour, size and look remain
editable. His quill, chat and memories save locally; no account or key is needed.

This is a friend-testing candidate. Please report the computer/OS and what happened
if anything breaks. Pause, Hide and Quit are available from the tray/menu bar.
