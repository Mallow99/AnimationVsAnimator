## Description

A little company, while you work.

Meet Cobalt, Amber, Moss, Violet and Ruby: five small personalities with things to
do and opinions about being thrown across your desktop. Invite one buddy or a whole
little group. Give them a book, a ball or somewhere comfortable to sit, then see
what they get up to.

- Pet, pick up and toss your buddies; they react, recover and remember.
- Give them furniture and objects. They read, draw, play games and spend time together.
- Change names, colours, size and personality. Mix stance, walking style, line/head style
  and smooth or pixel rendering, or start with a whole-look preset. Belongings and friendships persist.
- Join a game, start a familiar conversation or follow a branching story.
- Enable window climbing or playful mischief when you want it. Pause or hide everyone
  from the menu-bar / tray controls.

Includes cartoon sparring and slapstick: buddies can tumble or lose and reattach a limb.
There is no gore or permanent death; breakable limbs can be switched off in Settings.

Everything runs offline. No account, API key, subscription or model download.
Conversations use authored local dialogue with familiar topics and story choices;
this is a desktop toy rather than a general-purpose assistant.

## Before you buy

Target systems are Apple Silicon Macs and Windows 11 x64. Primary-monitor play only.
The Mac minimum binary target is macOS 13; publish the tested OS matrix with the
accepted release. Intel Macs, Windows ARM, Linux and phones are outside this edition.

Direct downloads are not Developer ID-notarized/certificate-signed. Your OS may show
an installation warning. Read the included installation guide before buying. No
Node or developer tools are needed; the native helper is included.

Updates are manual. Local saves stay on your computer. Optional desktop controls
are explained in Settings and start off. Refund requests follow itch.io's process.

Project direction: Mallow99. Developed with AI assistance. Independent original
desktop toy; no affiliation with animation franchises or other companion apps.
