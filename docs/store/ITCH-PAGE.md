# Mallow Desk Buddies

**A little company, while you work.**

Make room for a few tiny housemates. Give them a book, a ball or a comfortable
place to sit, then let them find their own fun on your desktop.

Start with one buddy or invite all five. They wander, read, draw, play, rest and
spend time together. Join in when you feel like it. They can keep themselves busy
when you don't.

## Little figures with lives of their own

- **Play together.** Pet, poke, pick up and toss your buddies. They react, recover
  and remember familiar moments.
- **Give them something to do.** Reading with saved bookmarks, drawing, dancing,
  catch, shared activities and cartoon sparring.
- **Make a little home.** Place a couch, chairs, lamp, TV, desk, bookshelf and more.
  Move the furniture and give each buddy their own belongings.
- **Let them play.** Pocket games, shared handheld sessions, TV runner games,
  Pong and Othello. The activity menu shows what each activity needs.
- **Say hello.** Built-in conversations cover familiar topics, remembered details
  and branching stories. Export a conversation or start a new one for one buddy.
- **Keep their progress.** Names, belongings, memories and friendships stay saved,
  even when you put a buddy away.

## Make each buddy yours

Choose a name, colour, size and personality. Pick inventive, competitive, gentle,
mischievous or adventurous, then adjust the details yourself.

Use a colour picker, enter an exact hex code, or slide the hue, saturation and
lightness controls. Adjust line thickness, head size and smooth or pixel rendering.
Movement has separate controls for stance, leg straightness, step length, foot
lift, arm motion, lean and bounce. Changes appear immediately and save per buddy.

Furniture has wood grain, woven upholstery and metal finishes. Books have textured
covers and paper pages. The controls use a quiet paper-and-linen finish.
You can also make custom items and furniture with local JSON definition files;
examples and a guide are included.

## Quiet when you need it

Window climbing, cursor play, window movement and sounds are optional and start
switched off on a fresh install. Pause everyone, hide the overlay or quit from
Settings or the menu-bar / tray controls.

There are no feeding timers or daily care chores to keep up with.

## Offline from the start

No account, API key, subscription or model download. No advertising or analytics.
Conversations and saves stay on your computer. The included dialogue is authored
for this toy: it covers familiar topics and story choices, rather than unrestricted
AI conversation. The customer edition does not connect to cloud AI or local models.

Automatic saves include last-good recovery and visible error messages if a write
fails. You can export a full backup. Updates are installed manually.

## Before you download

- **Platforms:** Apple Silicon Mac and Windows 11 x64. Primary-monitor play.
  The Mac binary targets macOS 13 or later. Intel Macs, Windows ARM, Linux and
  phones are outside this edition.
- **Installation:** extract the ZIP before opening the app. The native helper is
  included; you don't need Node or developer tools. Mac downloads are ad-hoc signed
  without Apple notarization; Windows downloads do not have a publisher certificate.
  Your OS may show a security warning. Read the included installation guide.
- **Desktop controls:** Mac window features may need Accessibility permission.
  Floor activities remain available with climbing switched off.
- **Cartoon slapstick:** sparring can include limbs coming off and being reattached.
  No gore or permanent death. You can switch breakable limbs off.
- **Custom art:** local JSON items and furniture are supported. An in-app art editor
  and custom character-image packs are not included.
